## Snapshot (verified, as of 2026-06-19)
- **Q1 2026 revenue:** $50.1M (+~1,065% YoY); FY2025 $50.7M (+605%); gross margin ~49% Q1-26.
- **Profitability:** Operating loss $(42.7)M in Q1-26; the +$362.8M "net income" is a **non-cash** warrant/deconsolidation artifact.
- **Balance sheet:** ~$1.48B cash + ST investments; minimal debt; **~$1.06B warrant liability**.
- **Dilution:** ~93M (YE2024) → ~496M shares (May 2026); >$1.8B raised since mid-2025.
- **Backlog:** ~$457M pro forma (Q1-26), heavily M&A-driven (Mistral).
- **Structure:** Ondas Networks **deconsolidated** Jan 16, 2026 (Ondas retains ~47.5%); OAS defense/autonomy is the core.
- **Flags:** J Capital short report (Feb 2026); Cramer "meme stock"; ~33% short interest; serial historical guidance misses.
