### 3.0 How to read this section (methodology & cautions)

- **Three-firm triangulation.** For each market we cite multiple firms because research-house estimates diverge enormously — often 3–6x — driven almost entirely by **scope definitions** (hardware-only vs. hardware+services; commercial-only vs. commercial+defense; narrow "anti-drone" vs. broad "C-UAS systems & integration"), not genuine disagreement about growth. We give ranges, not false-precision point estimates.
- **Global TAM ≠ what ONDS can serve.** TAM is the whole global market. ONDS's realistic **SAM** is gated by geography (it sells mainly to US + allied/NATO + Gulf + select APAC governments, not China/Russia bloc), product fit, and certification. **SOM** is what it can plausibly capture in ~3–5 years given a ~$50M (FY2025) → ≥$390M (FY2026 guided) revenue base and a $457M pro-forma backlog.
- **Double-counting is rampant here.** Drone-in-a-box, DaaS, drone inspection, and military drones are **nested subsets** of the broader UAS market — they must **not** be summed. C-UAS and stratospheric ISR are the only genuinely *additive* adjacencies. The master table flags every overlap.
- **Company-stated TAM.** Ondas's own 2026 investor materials claim a **~$117B total TAM** and **~$7.5B SAM (2025–2030)** across its served defense/security/autonomy markets, with a stated **$1.5B+ revenue / 30% EBITDA by 2030** goal (source: Ondas investor presentation via Quartr, https://quartr.com/events/ondas-inc-onds-investor-presentation_3eMC7aVI). We treat the $117B as a maximalist "sum-of-adjacencies" figure that **does** double-count; our independent build below is more conservative.
