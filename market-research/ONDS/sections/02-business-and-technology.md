## 2. Business Model, Segments & Technology

> **Important structural update (2026):** Ondas Inc. (NASDAQ: ONDS; formerly Ondas Holdings Inc.) has transformed dramatically. As of **January 16, 2026, Ondas deconsolidated Ondas Networks** following a capital restructuring of that entity (one-time non-cash gain of ~$51.5M), so Networks is no longer in Ondas' consolidated financials. The post-restructuring residual ownership/governance stake is **UNVERIFIED** from public sources. Meanwhile **Ondas Autonomous Systems (OAS)** has become the dominant growth engine, expanded via ~6 acquisitions in 2026 plus the pending **Cyberhawk** deal, and the company raised ~$1B in a January 2026 equity offering (ending Q1 2026 with **$1.48B cash/investments**). Historically, the company was framed as two segments; today it is effectively an OAS-centric defense/autonomy platform with Networks held as a separate (deconsolidated) entity. [Q1-2026 release; FY2025 release]

### 2.1 Corporate Structure & Segments

Historically two reportable segments:
- **(a) Ondas Networks** — proprietary software-defined wireless broadband (the **FullMAX** platform) for mission-critical IoT (MC-IoT) in rail, utilities, oil & gas, transportation, aviation and government. Revenue model: radio hardware + software/licensing, network buildouts via partners (Siemens Mobility). [ondas.com Company Overview; Networks PRs]
- **(b) Ondas Autonomous Systems (OAS)** — integrated unmanned/autonomous platforms across **air, ground, and stratospheric** domains: autonomous drones, counter-UAS (C-UAS), tactical ground robotics, loitering munitions, sensors, demining/engineering, and AI command software. OAS operates through a stack of operating companies (below). Revenue model: hardware/systems sales, programs, plus services (DaaS) and emerging recurring software/data. [FY2025 & Q1-2026 releases]

**OAS operating companies (as of Q1 2026):** American Robotics, Airobotics, Apeiro Motion, Roboteam, Sentrycs — described in the Q1-2026 release as providing "an integrated suite of autonomous aerial, ground, and counter-UAS solutions." Additional 2026 acquisitions named in the Q1 release: **Rotron Aerospace** (loitering munitions/propulsion), **Mistral Inc.** (US DoD prime contractor), **Bird Aerosystems / "Bird Aero"** (laser-based missile defense), **Indo-Earth** (military earth-moving/demining engineering equipment), and **World View** (stratospheric balloon ISR). [Q1-2026 release; 24/7 Wall St.]

### 2.2 Ondas Networks (FullMAX / dot-16 / rail)

- **FullMAX platform:** standards-based, **multi-patented, software-defined radio (SDR)** wireless broadband enabling **Mission-Critical IoT (MC-IoT)**, addressing bandwidth limits of legacy private licensed networks. Built on **IEEE 802.16s** and the newer **802.16t ("dot-16")** revision. [ondas.com; Networks PRs]
- **Standard / standards-body role:** Ondas Networks contributed proprietary IP to the **IEEE 802.16t** revision, working with **IEEE, the FCC, the AAR (Association of American Railroads), Class I railroads and railroad suppliers**. Equipment on the 802.16-2017 baseline is **software-upgradable** to the new revision — a key SDR moat. [802.16t ratification PR, Mar 2023]
- **Rail / 900 MHz "A Block":** Ondas completed the **first 802.16 buildout on the new 900 MHz "A Block"** for a major **Class I railroad in Chicago**, upgrading core base-station infrastructure as a mission-critical backbone, supporting the industry's migration off the **retiring legacy 900 MHz network**. Previously announced plans to upgrade Chicago's **Metra** commuter rail to 802.16 on the A Block. Use cases: PTC backhaul, wayside infrastructure, advanced defect detection. [Chicago buildout PR]
- **Siemens relationship:** **Siemens Mobility** is the named partner — Ondas + Siemens jointly deliver the rail migration plan, pairing FullMAX with Siemens Mobility networking equipment. (Siemens Mobility PR filed as 8-K exhibit, Dec 5, 2024.) [SEC ex99-1]
- **Customers / end markets:** Class I railroads (unnamed), Metra, plus targeted utilities, oil & gas, transportation, aviation and government. [Networks PRs; ondas.com]

### 2.3 Ondas Autonomous Systems (OAS)

**American Robotics — Scout System (drone-in-a-box):** AI-driven "drone-in-a-box" for continuous remote operation; **first FAA-approved drone system for automated BVLOS without a human operator on-site (2021)**. A later **Part 107 waiver** expands automated BVLOS over people and moving vehicles from a Remote Operations Center, with ranges up to **~10 miles (16 km)** for linear assets (pipelines, rail, transmission lines). [BVLOS waiver PRs; DroneLife; Commercial UAV News]

**Airobotics — Optimus System (drone-in-a-box):** Industrial-grade platform = **Optimus drone + Optimus Airbase**, with **robotic battery swap and robotic payload swap** for near-continuous 24/7 autonomous flight; one airbase covers up to ~30 sq mi. Markets: **Smart City, Public Safety (Drone-as-a-First-Responder/DFR), Infrastructure, Defense.** **Optimus 1-EX received historic U.S. FAA Type Certification (Sept 6, 2023)** — the first non-air-carrier drone designed for autonomous security/data capture to be type-certified (4-year process; enables flight over people). FAA also approved a **noise certification standard** for Optimus. [Optimus 1-EX TC PR; noise cert PR]

**Counter-UAS:**
- **Iron Drone Raider** (Airobotics): autonomous **net-based kinetic interceptor**. On detecting a hostile drone it auto-launches a hyper-fast interceptor, locks on via proprietary AI vision, and neutralizes the target with a **net + parachute** for safe takedown in populated/sensitive areas. Accelerated to meet **IDF** requirements; deployed/ordered in Europe and the Middle East. [Iron Drone Raider PRs; Breaking Defense]
- **Sentrycs** (acquired 2026): **Cyber-over-RF (CoRF)** C-UAS operating at the communication-protocol layer — detect, identify, locate and **take control** of rogue drones **without jamming or spoofing**; serves Tier-1 defense/security agencies across **25+ countries**. [Sentrycs PRs]

**Ground robotics & other 2026 additions:** **Roboteam** (combat-proven rugged tactical UGVs for EOD/ISR), **Apeiro Motion** (ground robotics + tethered UAV), plus **Rotron** (loitering munitions/propulsion), **Mistral** (US DoD prime), **Bird Aero** (laser missile defense), **Indo-Earth** (demining/earth-moving), **World View** (stratospheric ISR). [Q1-2026 release]

**Eurosatory 2026 — "Autonomy at First Contact":** Launched a **system-of-systems** architecture across five categories — Air Defense, Aerial Intelligence, Aerial One-Way Attack, Ground Robotics, AI Software. New named systems: **Iron Wave, Dual Shield, MODUS, Scout Cyber-over-RF, Iron Arrow, LADOS.** Highlighted the **ONBERG** European JV for local manufacturing/support. [Eurosatory 2026 PR]

**Cyberhawk acquisition (~$125M, announced June 2026, expected close Q3 2026):** Cyberhawk = global leader in **drone-based critical-infrastructure inspection + AI visual data management**. Flagship cloud-native **iHawk** platform (digital twins, geospatial intelligence, AI analytics). Inspected **>500,000 assets**. Blue-chip customers: **PG&E, Southern California Edison, Shell, SSE, ESB, Qatar Energy, Bechtel.** Terms: ~95% cash + ~$5M leadership stock rollover. Financials: **>$45M FY27 revenue, ~95% recurring, ~$95M backlog**, EBITDA margins trending toward **25%+ by 2030**. Rationale: build a category-defining autonomous intelligence platform and pivot toward **recurring software/data revenue**. [Cyberhawk PR; Benzinga]

### 2.4 Business-Model Economics
- **Q1 2026:** Revenue **$50.1M** (+~1,065% YoY vs $4.3M; +66% QoQ); **gross margin 49%** (up from 35%). Driven by C-UAS, ground systems, sensor systems. **Backlog $457M pro forma** (vs $68.3M end-2025, +570%). **$1.48B cash/investments.** FY2026 guide **≥$390M** (~670% YoY). [Q1-2026 release]
- **Profitability path:** Product companies hit **adjusted-EBITDA profitability in Q1 2026** (6 months early); OAS adj-EBITDA profitability pulled forward to **Q1 2027**; company-wide adj-EBITDA profitability targeted **Q1 2028.** [Q1-2026 release]
- **Revenue mix:** Predominantly hardware/systems + defense programs today; **recurring/DaaS/software** is the strategic pivot (Cyberhawk ~95% recurring; DFR/DaaS). [Q1-2026 release; Cyberhawk PR]

### 2.6 Moat Assessment
Most defensible assets are **regulatory/certification** (FAA Type Cert + BVLOS firsts) and **standards-embedded networking IP** (patented FullMAX inside IEEE 802.16t with AAR/rail lock-in). The OAS defense portfolio is increasingly assembled via **M&A rather than purely organic IP**, so part of the moat is **integration + breadth (system-of-systems) + combat-proven references + geographic licenses (25+ countries)**. The **emerging data moat** (Cyberhawk's proprietary inspection dataset + iHawk, ~95% recurring) is the most attractive long-term economic moat but is **pending close (Q3 2026)**. Caveats: heavy reliance on **M&A and a large 2026 capital raise**, not yet company-level profitable (targeted 2028), customers often **undisclosed**, and Networks now **deconsolidated**.
