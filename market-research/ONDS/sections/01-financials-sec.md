### Governance / controls
- **No restatement. No auditor change** (Rosenberg Rich Baker Berman since 2018; Exhibit 16.1 relates to the Jan 2026 Nevada reorganization, not a dismissal).
- **No material weakness** — Item 9A: disclosure controls and ICFR effective at YE2025. Management's ICFR assessment **excluded six 2025 acquisitions** (~36% of assets, ~53% of revenue) — a permitted post-acquisition exclusion to integrate in FY2026.
- **Ondas Networks deconsolidation (1/16/2026):** lost control after the Networks Series B financing; retains **~47.5%** (equity method, fair-value option); $51.5M gain recognized. Reported revenue is essentially all OAS from Q1 2026.
