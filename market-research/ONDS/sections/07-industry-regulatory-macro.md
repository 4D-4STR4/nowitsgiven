## 7. Industry, Regulatory & Macro Lateral Factors

**As of 2026-06-19.** Tailwinds and headwinds that move ONDS independent of company execution.

### 7.1 The "friendly supply-chain" regulatory stack (TAILWIND — the core structural bull case)
A reinforcing set of US rules systematically excludes Chinese drones (DJI/Autel) and channels demand to NDAA-compliant US makers. Ondas/American Robotics sits inside every gate.

| Layer | Status | What it does | Relevance to ONDS |
|-------|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| **NDAA §848 (FY2020)** | ENACTED | Bars DoD from procuring UAS w/ critical components from China (later Russia/Iran/NK) | DoD can only buy compliant UAS |
| **American Security Drone Act (ASDA)** | ENACTED (PL 118-31, Dec 2023); operating ban effective Dec 22, 2025; FAR 52.240-1 in contracts since Nov 12, 2024 | Extends ban **government-wide** (agencies, contractors, grant recipients) | Closes entire federal drone market to Chinese makers |
| **§1709 (FY2025 NDAA) → FCC Covered List** | ENACTED; **FCC added foreign-made UAS to Covered List Dec 22, 2025** | New DJI/Autel models can't get FCC authorization → can't be sold in US | Reaches the **commercial** market, not just gov |
| **EO 14307 "Unleashing American Drone Dominance"** | SIGNED Jun 6, 2025 | "Prioritize US-made UAS"; expand Blue UAS list; accelerate BVLOS rule | Buy-American-drones mandate + Blue List on-ramp |
| **EO 14305 "Restoring American Airspace Sovereignty"** | SIGNED Jun 6, 2025 | C-UAS task force; **DOJ/DHS grants for state/local C-UAS buys** | Funds demand for Iron Drone Raider + Sentrycs |
| **Blue UAS (DCMA) / Green UAS (AUVSI)** | Programs | Pre-vetted compliant-UAS lists = procurement fast-track | **Optimus added to Blue UAS Cleared List Jan 28, 2026** (after Green UAS 2024–25) |

Sources: congress.gov S.473 (ASDA); federalregister.gov FAR 52.240-1; dronelife.com (FCC Covered List Dec 22, 2025); whitehouse.gov EO 14307; ir.ondas.com/press-releases/detail/275 (Blue UAS). **Flags:** FCC determination legal basis partly ambiguous (determination vs auto-trigger); OMB memo M-26-02 contents not fully reviewed.

### 7.2 Demand drivers (TAILWIND)
- **Counter-UAS demand surge:** drone incursions over airports/bases, Ukraine + Middle East conflicts driving global C-UAS spending; EO 14305 grant funding for state/local agencies.
- **Defense-tech funding boom:** private defense-tech investment **>$14.6B in first 5 months of 2026** (vs $9.6B full-year 2025, $1.6B in 2020). Anduril $5B Series H at **$61B**; Palantir FY25 revenue +56% to $4.4B. Rising tide for ONDS-type valuations and capital access.
- **BVLOS / Part 108:** DOT/FAA issued proposed Part 108 BVLOS rule **Aug 7, 2025** (final targeted within 240 days of EO). Unlocks scaled, no-pilot-on-site autonomous operations central to American Robotics/Airobotics.

### 7.3 Macro / financial conditions (MIXED)
- **Fed funds 3.50–3.75%**, held at June 17, 2026 FOMC (first meeting under new Chair Kevin Warsh); reached via 3 consecutive cuts since Sep 2025. June dot plot turned **hawkish** (~3.8% 2026 median, fewer cuts ahead). [usbank.com; tradingkey.com]
- **Russell 2000 +~16.3% YTD 2026 at records** — "great rotation" into small caps; capital-raising window for dilutive defense-tech names is **wide open** right now. BUT: 41–46% of R2000 companies can't cover interest from operating profit; **$368B 2026 maturity wall** — ONDS sits in this fragile cohort. [ibtimes.com.au]
- **Interpretation:** favorable raise environment today; a risk-off turn (hawkish Fed, geopolitical de-escalation removing the defense bid) would close that window fast for the weakest names.

### 7.4 Industry structure / consolidation (HEADWIND for sub-scale players)
- **Axon → Dedrone** (closed Oct 2, 2024; value undisclosed) — C-UAS consolidation.
- **AeroVironment → BlueHalo** (~$4.1B all-stock, closed May 2025; pro forma rev >$1.7B) — scaled "next-gen defense tech" spanning UAS/C-UAS/space/cyber/directed energy.
- **"System of systems" trend:** procurement favors integrated stacks + lead-system-integrators over point solutions (e.g., Perennial Autonomy **$500M C-UAS IDIQ**, May 2026 — largest single C-UAS award). Pentagon's reported ~$54B autonomous-warfare bet.
- **Shakeout thesis (Anduril co-founder Trae Stephens, Jun 2026):** "a couple of new credible players, the rest is noise"; mid-stage defense startups at "50–200x" multiples vs industry 2–2.5x revenue — "disconnected from reality"; predicts consolidation, few survivors. *(Interest-conflicted opinion, but well-sourced.)*
