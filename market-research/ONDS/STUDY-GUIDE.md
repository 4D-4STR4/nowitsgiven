## 🧠 The thesis in one breath
A serial-diluting micro-cap reinvented itself in 2026 as a **defense / counter-drone autonomy roll-up** — buying ~6 companies with a ~$1B raise to turn ~$7M revenue into a ~$390M-guided, ~$5B-market-cap story. Growth is **real but bought, not yet proven organic.** The whole game: *do the A's (growth, catalysts) outrun the F's (dilution, weak track record)?*
