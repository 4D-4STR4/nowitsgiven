# Project Vision — Working Title: **SALVAGE**

> A 1–4 player co-op **derelict-salvage extraction roguelite**.
> Board procedurally-generated space wrecks, scavenge valuable salvage under
> escalating threats, and *extract before the wreck dies*. Spend your haul at a
> home base to upgrade gear, ship, and crew — then jump deeper into the void.

## The bet
Tiny / AI-assisted team. Lean budget. We are deliberately targeting the proven,
high-ROI lane that **Lethal Company, Phasmophobia, R.E.P.O., Content Warning,
and Deep Rock Galactic** have validated:

- **Co-op-core, but fully fun solo** — tension and mastery alone; chaos and
  comedy together.
- **Streamable by design** — proximity voice + emergent moments = free
  marketing via Twitch/TikTok clips.
- **Cheap to produce, distinctive to look at** — a low-fi, high-atmosphere art
  style that reads as intentional, not under-resourced.
- **Endlessly replayable** — procedural levels + roguelite meta-progression =
  "just one more run."
- **Single code path for SP & MP** — solo is "host with one player," so co-op
  is never a painful retrofit.

## Design pillars (provisional — refined by GDD)
1. **Risk you can feel.** Every second deeper is more loot and more danger. The
   extraction timer is the heartbeat.
2. **The wreck is the enemy.** Structure, oxygen, power, and darkness are
   threats before any monster is. Environmental dread > jump scares.
3. **Better together, not punished alone.** Roles and logistics reward teamwork
   without gating solo play.
4. **Emergent stories, not scripted ones.** Systems collide to make moments
   players *have* to clip and share.
