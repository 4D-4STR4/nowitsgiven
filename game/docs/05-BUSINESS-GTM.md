# Business, Pricing & Go-To-Market Plan — SALVAGE

_Source: business/GTM research agent._

> **TL;DR:** Premium one-time purchase at **$14.99 in Early Access → $19.99 at 1.0**. Build to
> **20k–40k wishlists** pre-launch, spent during your one-and-only Next Fest. Treat the game as
> **streamer-fuel** (the genre's growth engine) and spend the tiny budget on a demo + creator
> seeding, not ads. Cosmetic-only MTX *later*, never pay-for-power. Break-even ≈ **2k–6k net
> copies** — well below even the pessimistic case.

---

## 1. Monetization model — Premium, cheap, co-op (NOT F2P)

Every money-maker in this lane is premium and cheap:

| Game | Model | Price | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lethal Company | Premium EA | $9.99 | ~10M+ copies (solo dev), ~$33.6M net |
| R.E.P.O. | Premium EA | $9.99 | ~15M copies, best-selling Steam game of 2025 |
| Content Warning | Premium (1 free day) | $7.99 | 6.6M free → 2.2M+ paid in 2 months |
| Deep Rock Galactic | Premium + cosmetic DLC | $29.99 | 8M+ units, free seasons |

The revenue multiplier in co-op is **the friction of getting 4 friends to buy together** — a low
premium price minimizes it. F2P would force live-service monetization infra a tiny team can't
sustain, and the hits prove you don't need it. One buyer dragging in 3 friends = ~$60/friend-group
with zero monetization ops.

**Price:** EA **$14.99** (justified above the $9.99 floor by solo-viability + roguelite depth);
1.0 **$19.99** (~33% bump, standard EA→1.0 practice, creates "buy now before it rises" urgency).
Note Steam rules: no discount within 30 days of a price increase; announce the bump 2–4 weeks ahead.

**Regional pricing:** accept Steam's PPP-based recommendations everywhere — crucial for co-op so
friend-groups in Brazil/Argentina/Turkey/SEA can all buy in.

**Cosmetic MTX — later and clean (the DRG model):** ship the game, bank goodwill, *then* add
cosmetic-only packs. Never pay-for-power, no lootboxes, generous free earn-track, no launch cash
shop. Optional all-cosmetic "Supporter Pack" ($4.99) after reviews go Very Positive.

## 2. Revenue projections

Assumptions: Steam 30%; blended **~$10 net/copy** lifetime (mix of EA/1.0/regional/sale); units =
lifetime paid through ~12 months post-EA. Co-op WL→sales is right-skewed (10× below to 20× above
median).

| Scenario | Lifetime paid units | Net revenue (@$10) | Narrative |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pessimistic** | 5,000 | **$50,000** | ~10–15k WL, decent reviews, little streamer pickup |
| **Base** | 30,000 | **$300,000** | ~25–35k WL, Very Positive, modest streamer flywheel |
| **Optimistic** | 150,000+ | **$1.5M+** | Mid/large streamers catch fire, TikTok spread, friend-multiplier |
| **Tail (unplannable)** | 1M+ | $10M+ | Full R.E.P.O./Lethal virality — build for it, don't budget for it |

**Break-even** (at $10 net/copy): $20k budget → 2,000 copies; $40k → 4,000; $60k → 6,000.
Even the pessimistic 5,000-unit case clears a $20–40k budget; the base case returns 5–15×.

## 3. Wishlist & launch strategy

**Target 20,000–40,000 wishlists pre-launch** (floor 10k, stretch 50k+) — the band to hit Steam's
**Popular Upcoming** front-page slot and make Next Fest pay.

- **Coming Soon page live 6–9 months out** — #1 wishlist asset; every clip/post points here.
- **Tags:** `Co-op`, `Online Co-Op`, `Survival Horror`, `Roguelite`, `Extraction`, `Space`,
  `Multiplayer`, `Singleplayer` — tags drive the recommendation algorithm.
- **Capsule art = highest-ROI asset.** Design the *small* capsule first (legible as a thumbnail),
  one focal point/mood, "creepy space wreck + scavenge tension" at a glance. Worth paying a freelancer.
- **Trailer:** gameplay in first 5s (Steam autoplays silently), lead with feeling, <60s, plus a
  separate 15–30s vertical cut for TikTok/Shorts.
- **Next Fest (the biggest free lever):** you get ONE, ever — spend it on the *last* fest before
  launch. Build the audience first, use the fest to multiply it (pre-fest WL correlates with fest
  gains at r=0.825). **Release the demo weeks/months before the fest, not during.** 2026 fests:
  Feb 23–Mar 2, Jun 15–22, Oct 19–26.
- **Launch with momentum:** Coming Soon early → early demo → seed creators → last Next Fest → land
  in Popular Upcoming → announce date + "price rises later" → launch Tue–Thu, avoid AAA weeks/sale
  middles → wishlist email blast → strong day-1 concurrency → algorithm rewards you. First 24–72h
  set months of algorithm treatment.

## 4. Marketing on a shoestring ($0–$2k)

**This genre is the most streamer-fuel-efficient on Steam** — Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Content
Warning were *streamed into existence*, not advertised. (~41 hours-watched ≈ 1 copy sold; multiplayer
shows the strongest stream→wishlist correlation.)

- **Free keys to small/mid creators (1k–100k followers), not the top** — send 4-packs (it's co-op),
  personalized. Free and highest-leverage.
- **Build in public** on TikTok/Shorts/Reels + devlog — repetition, not one announcement. A single
  good vertical clip has driven 1,000–2,000 wishlists.
- **The demo IS the marketing** — free, streamable, out early for organic discovery + Next Fest signal.
- **Discord from day one** — community HQ, playtest pool, and "find a squad" LFG (co-op games die
  without people to play with).
- **Reddit** (r/IndieGaming, r/CoOpGaming, genre subs) — lead with a GIF, participate as a human.
- **Cash:** ~$1,000–1,500 on capsule artist + trailer edit; ~$300–500 on key-distribution tooling
  (Keymailer/Lurkit). **Do NOT** buy display/paid ads.

## 5. Store page copy (draft)

**Title:** *SALVAGE* is clean but SEO-crowded; stronger: **DERELICT**, *Scavenger Protocol*,
*Wreckless*, *Deadweight*, *Hulk Divers*, *Salvage: Deep Black*. Recommendation: **DERELICT** or
SALVAGE + subtitle.

**Short hook:** *Loot dead starships. Extract before something finds you. Bring friends — or don't.*

**About:**
> You and up to three friends are salvage crews working the deadest, darkest corner of space. Board
> derelict starships, strip them for everything valuable, and get back before your oxygen, your
> nerve, or your luck runs out. Something is already aboard. It heard you dock.
>
> A 1–4 player co-op extraction roguelite about greed and survival. Every wreck is procedurally
> generated, every run a gamble, every credit you extract feeds a deep meta-progression of better
> gear, ships, and crew. Push deeper for the big haul — or cut your losses and live to scavenge
> another day. Play solo as tense survival-horror, or bring a crew for pure co-op chaos.

**Feature bullets:** 1–4 player co-op or fully solo · extraction-or-death · procedural derelicts ·
roguelite meta-progression · survival-horror tension built for streaming (proximity comms) ·
easy to learn, brutal to master.

## 6. Early Access plan

EA buys **funding + feedback + algorithm/reviews** (two visibility events instead of one).
- **EA-launch bar:** core loop genuinely fun on day one (board → scavenge → hunted → extract →
  spend → repeat), 3–4 wreck types, a few enemies, working meta spine, solid co-op netcode. Do NOT
  ship a boring/broken loop "to fix later" — the #1 EA killer.
- **Duration:** 9–14 months (median 2025 graduate ≈ 437 days); don't drift past ~18 months.
- **Avoid EA-fatigue death** (only ~20% of 2025 graduates did better at 1.0 than in EA): **withhold
  a content splash for 1.0** (biomes/boss/mode), ship predictable cadence, publish an honest roadmap,
  time 1.0 as a deliberate marketing event near a sale.

---

## 7. Community & retention

Discord at the heart (LFG/squad-finder mandatory for co-op longevity). Small patches every 1–2
weeks, named content update every 6–8 weeks (predictability > size — going quiet kills co-op EA
games). Content drops = re-launch marketing events. Seasonal events (Halloween free spooky cosmetic
+ event wreck) tied to Steam sales. Free cosmetic earn-track; spotlight community clips.

---

## 8. Launch-week ops (condensed)

- **T-2–4 wks:** announce date + "price rises at 1.0"; final wishlist push; send 4-pack keys to
  seeded creators (streams live on launch day); pre-write announcements; stress-test netcode.
- **Launch day (Tue–Thu):** go live, verify price/region/EA tag; publish announcement (re-pings
  wishlisters); all hands in Discord/forums; protect the first 10–50 reviews; same-day hotfix
  crash/MP bugs; amplify every streamer.
- **+1–3 days:** day-1/2 hotfix (signals active devs + re-triggers visibility); acknowledge top
  bugs; protect the Very Positive threshold.
- **+1 wk:** "thank you + what's next" roadmap post; second creator key wave; lock update cadence;
  retro WL→sales vs model.
